---
description: Control de-kaku (status, toggle on/off, intercepts, stats, theme)
---

Jalankan tool MCP de-kaku berikut sesuai argumen:
- `status` → dekaku_status
- `on` → dekaku_toggle mode=on
- `off` → dekaku_toggle mode=off
- `intercepts` → dekaku_intercepts limit=20
- `stats` → dekaku_stats
- `theme <nama>` → dekaku_theme theme=<nama>

Contoh: `/dekaku off`, `/dekaku stats`, `/dekaku theme dekaku-purple`.
